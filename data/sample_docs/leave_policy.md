# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Harbourline Software Ltd, a fictional company used for demonstration.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued from the start date. Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis. Leave requests longer than five consecutive days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance through the HR portal. Up to five unused days can be carried over into the next leave year.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave each year after six months of continuous employment. Sick leave can also be used to care for a dependant who is unwell. A medical certificate may be requested for absences of three or more consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Employees who have worked for the company for at least twelve months can take up to 26 weeks of parental leave. The company tops up government parental leave payments to 80 percent of the employee's normal salary for the first 12 weeks.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to three days of paid bereavement leave on the death of a close family member, and one day for other people they had a close relationship with.
