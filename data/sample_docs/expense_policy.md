# Expense Policy

Harbourline Software Ltd reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay for personally.

## Submitting Claims
Expense claims must be submitted through the finance system within 30 days of the purchase. Every claim needs an itemised receipt. Claims under 50 dollars can be approved by a team lead; claims of 50 dollars or more require approval from a department manager.

## Travel
Domestic flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel portal. Employees travelling overnight can claim up to 220 dollars per night for accommodation and a daily meal allowance of 75 dollars. Taxis and rideshares are reimbursed when public transport is not practical.

## Home Office Equipment
Each employee can claim up to 600 dollars every two years for home office equipment such as a chair, monitor or desk. Equipment bought with this allowance belongs to the employee.

## Non-reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic and parking fines, and upgrades to premium travel classes are not reimbursed.
