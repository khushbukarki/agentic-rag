# IT Security Policy

All staff and contractors at Harbourline Software Ltd must follow this policy.

## Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and the cloud console. Staff must use the company password manager to store credentials.

## Devices
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within seven days of release. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## Data Handling
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Production databases can only be accessed through the approved bastion host. Confidential documents must be shared with internal links rather than as email attachments.

## Reporting Incidents
Suspected phishing emails should be reported with the report-phish button in the mail client. Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team immediately through the incident channel.
